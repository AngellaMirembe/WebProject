About Nyakasura School

Excellence • Discipline • Leadership

Our History

Nyakasura School, located in Fort Portal, Western Uganda, was founded in 1926 by Scottish Commander Ernest Ebohard Calwell. Famous for its Scottish traditions—including boys wearing kilts as uniforms—the historic institution has been a pioneer in Ugandan education, generating its own electricity and water long before modern infrastructure reached the region. The school has a rich legacy of academic excellence, discipline, and leadership, producing notable alumni who have made significant contributions to Uganda and beyond. With its unique blend of Scottish heritage and Ugandan culture, Nyakasura School continues to be a beacon of quality education and character development in the heart of Fort Portal.

School Uniform

Boys' Uniform

The boys' uniform at Nyakasura School is iconic and steeped in tradition. It consists of a kilt, which is a nod to the school's Scottish heritage, paired with a white shirt and a blazer. The kilt is typically made of tartan fabric, reflecting the school's unique identity. This distinctive uniform not only promotes a sense of pride and belonging among the students but also serves as a symbol of the school's commitment to discipline and excellence. Although once in A level, the boys wear trousers, the kilt remains a central part of the school's identity and is worn by students in lower classes, reinforcing the school's rich cultural heritage and fostering a strong sense of community among its students.

Girls' Uniform

The girls' uniform at Nyakasura School is designed to be both practical and respectful of the school's traditions. It typically consists of a skirt, blouse, and blazer, often in the same tartan pattern as the boys' kilts. The uniform promotes a sense of unity and discipline among the students while also allowing for comfort and ease of movement.

Identity & Pride

The uniform is not only a symbol of the school's heritage but also fosters a strong sense of identity and pride among the students. It serves as a daily reminder of the school's values and traditions, encouraging students to uphold the standards of excellence and discipline that Nyakasura School is known for.

Culture & Heritage

Nyakasura culture is a blend of Scottish traditions and rich Tooro Kingdom folklore. Based in Fort Portal in Western Uganda, it centers around the iconic Nyakasura School, known for its iconic all-boys kilt uniform, and the mystical Amabere Ga Nyinamwiru caves and crater lakes. The school fosters academic excellence, discipline, and leadership, while the caves and lakes are steeped in local legends of love and tragedy. Together, they create a unique cultural tapestry that celebrates education, heritage, and the enduring spirit of the Tooro people. Visitors can explore the school’s history and the enchanting natural wonders that define Nyakasura’s identity.
