About Nyakasura School

Excellence • Discipline • Leadership

Our History

Nyakasura School, located in Fort Portal, Western Uganda, was founded in 1926 by Scottish Commander Ernest Ebohard Calwell. Famous for its Scottish traditions—including boys wearing kilts as uniforms—the historic institution has been a pioneer in Ugandan education, generating its own electricity and water long before modern infrastructure reached the region. The school has a rich legacy of academic excellence, discipline, and leadership, producing notable alumni who have made significant contributions to Uganda and beyond. With its unique blend of Scottish heritage and Ugandan culture, Nyakasura School continues to be a beacon of quality education and character development in the heart of Fort Portal.

Administration

Nyakasura School is led by a dedicated team of administrators who are committed to upholding the school's values and ensuring a high standard of education for all students. The administration works closely with teachers, students, and parents to create a supportive and nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence, discipline, and leadership development. With a focus on holistic education, the administration strives to provide resources and opportunities that enable students to thrive both academically and personally.

School Uniform

Boys' Uniform

The boys' uniform at Nyakasura School is iconic and steeped in tradition. It consists of a kilt, which is a nod to the school's Scottish heritage, paired with a white shirt and a blazer. The kilt is typically made of tartan fabric, reflecting the school's unique identity. This distinctive uniform not only promotes a sense of pride and belonging among the students but also serves as a symbol of the school's commitment to discipline and excellence. Although once in A level, the boys wear trousers, the kilt remains a central part of the school's identity and is worn by students in lower classes, reinforcing the school's rich cultural heritage and fostering a strong sense of community among its students.

Girls' Uniform

The girls' uniform at Nyakasura School is designed to be both practical and respectful of the school's traditions. It typically consists of a skirt, blouse, and blazer, often in the same tartan pattern as the boys' kilts. The uniform promotes a sense of unity and discipline among the students while also allowing for comfort and ease of movement.

Identity & Pride

The uniform is not only a symbol of the school's heritage but also fosters a strong sense of identity and pride among the students. It serves as a daily reminder of the school's values and traditions, encouraging students to uphold the standards of excellence and discipline that Nyakasura School is known for.

Our Pillars

1. Fear of God

At Nyakasura School, we instill in our students a deep respect for the divine and a strong moral compass. The fear of God is a fundamental pillar that guides our students to lead ethical lives, make responsible decisions, and contribute positively to society. We believe that fostering spiritual growth and moral integrity is essential in shaping well-rounded individuals who can navigate the complexities of the world with wisdom and compassion.

2. Hard Work

Hard work is a core value at Nyakasura School, where we encourage our students to put in their best effort in all aspects of their education and personal development. We believe that success is achieved through dedication, perseverance, and a strong work ethic. By fostering a culture of hard work, we prepare our students to overcome challenges, achieve their goals, and make meaningful contributions to their communities and the world at large.

3. Talent Development

The school is committed to nurturing the unique talents and abilities of each student. We provide a supportive environment that encourages creativity, innovation, and self-expression. Through a variety of extracurricular activities, clubs, and programs, we help students discover and develop their passions, whether in academics, sports, arts, or leadership. Our goal is to empower students to reach their full potential and make a positive impact in their chosen fields.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To produce students who are God-fearing, servant leaders, intellectually competent, solution-oriented, devoted, and physically/morally upright to meet the specific and general needs of society in a global, modern, and pluralistic world.

Our Vision

To be a co-education school providing balanced quality secondary education in Africa.
