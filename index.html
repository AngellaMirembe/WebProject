About Us

Nyakasura School, located in the scenic town of Fort Portal, is a prestigious institution committed to providing quality education and holistic development for students. With a rich history and a tradition of excellence, we strive to nurture young minds and prepare them for the challenges of the future. Our dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art facilities, and vibrant community create an environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and personally.
