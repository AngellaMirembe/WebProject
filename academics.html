Academics

Excellence in Education

Programs Offered:

Sciences

We provide a strong foundation in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. With well equipped laboratories, students can explore scientific concepts through hands-on learning.

Arts

Developing creativity and critical thinking through arts subjects. Field trips and performances enhance the learning experience, giving learners immersive learning environments and active engagement.

ICT

Digital literacy and technology skills are crucial in today's ever-changing world and we recognize that. Our ICT program prepares students to leverage technology for learning and innovation.

Curriculum

Nyakasura School follows the Uganda National Curriculum, enriched with additional subjects and activities to prepare students for higher education and life. We emphasize holistic development through academics, sports, arts, and community service.

Academic Facilities

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped labs for practical experiments and hands-on learning.

Library

A comprehensive library with books, journals, and digital resources.

Computer Lab

Modern computers and software for ICT education.

Academic Achievements

Our students consistently excel in national examinations and competitions, securing places in top universities and winning awards in various fields.
